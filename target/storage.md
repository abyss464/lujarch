# Storage

For each app the owner can set how much of the phone's storage it may keep for itself.

## An app's storage

### STO-1 An app's storage has the limit the owner sets

- Home: Settings > Apps > (app) > Storage & cache > Storage limit
- Switch: Use storage limit; Storage: a size; When it is full: Clear its cache first, Stop it saving
- Default: off; once turned on, Storage 4 GB, Clear its cache first
- Verify: phone

With Use storage limit on, the app's User data and Cache together hold at most Storage. When the
app reaches it, Clear its cache first clears its cache and the app saves on into the room that
frees; once it has no cache left, and at once with Stop it saving, it cannot save more, as when the
phone's storage is full, and keeps what it holds. Files it saves to the phone's shared storage
outside its own folders (Download, pictures and other media) are not counted, and the page says
so. Every size from 100 MB to the phone's storage can be set, also one below what the app holds
now.

Storage & cache shows Storage limit with the app's use against it. The page shows its User data
and Cache against Storage; whether it is within its limit or full, with since when; and when its
cache was last cleared for its limit.
