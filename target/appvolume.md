# App volume

Each app that plays media has its own volume, a share of the Media volume, set in the volume panel
under Media.

## Volume

### VOL-1 Each playing app has its own share of the Media volume

- Home: the volume panel > Media > (apps playing)
- Switch: each app: its volume, 0 to 100 % of Media
- Default: 100 % for every app
- Verify: vm

Under Media, the volume panel shows a line with the icons of the apps playing now and how many they
are; it opens to one slider per app, which stays until the panel closes, and the line stays open or
folded as the owner left it. An app's volume scales only that app's media, games, speech and
assistant sound, never calls, ringing, notifications, alarms or system sounds; Media stays the
master and an app's volume only takes away. The volume follows the app across restarts and updates
of the phone and the app, and ends when the app is uninstalled. Only apps have a volume of their
own; the system's own sounds do not.
