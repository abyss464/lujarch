# Motion

Lujarch's own moments move in one language made from its mark in motion (BRD-3): shards, the
mark as a jaw of ten shards, water and one key light from the upper left, in red ochre on black.
Every other animation is Android's. Each moment that has a switch plays Android's own animation while it is
off, and with Remove animations on every moment jumps to its end.

## Starting

### MOT-1 The phone starts with the mark falling into water

- Home: none
- Switch: none
- Default: none
- Verify: vm

While the phone starts, an arrow falls on a line onto its reflection and strikes the water; its ten
shards splash up and gather into the mark, which then turns, sways and bites until the start is
done, and fades out as it keeps turning.

## Lock screen

### MOT-2 The lock screen's fingerprint is the mark

- Home: Settings > Accessibility > Color & motion > Lujarch motion
- Switch: Lock screen and fingerprint: on, off
- Default: on
- Verify: phone

The mark sits on the fingerprint sensor at its size, lit and breathing. A finger parts its jaws
round the sensor's light; while the finger rests, rings of water leave it. A match bites the jaws
shut with a flash, bursts the shards and sends a wave that uncovers the home screen; a mismatch
shakes the jaws and closes them when the finger lifts. The screen turns off under a rising
waterline and wakes as the water drains off it from the top. On always-on display the mark is
dimly lit and still.

### MOT-3 The lock screen's media is a circle until opened

- Home: the lock screen
- Switch: the media's circle: open, folded
- Default: folded
- Verify: vm

On the lock screen the playing media shows as a circle with its app's icon; a tap grows Android's
media card out of it, a tap on the card's app icon folds it back, and it stays as the owner left
it. With several players the circle shows the app of the card that would open.

## Charging

### MOT-4 Plugging in fills the mark like a battery

- Home: Settings > Accessibility > Color & motion > Lujarch motion
- Switch: Charging: on, off
- Default: on
- Verify: phone

When a charger is plugged in, a wave leaves the port (or wells up from the center for wireless
charging) and lifts the mark from the sensor between the clock and the charging text, twice its
size; one shard is lit for each 10 % of charge, the one charging fills from below, the mark bites
once and sinks back onto the sensor, where it stays a gauge while the phone charges. Android's
clock pulse and charging text ride the wave.

## System bars and gestures

### MOT-5 Privacy indicators open round the camera

- Home: Settings > System > Status bar > Status bar items > Privacy indicators
- Switch: Privacy ring (SB-2)
- Default: on
- Verify: vm

When an app starts using the camera or the microphone, the ten shards rise out of the camera
hole's rim in the privacy color beside the sensor's icon, then shrink to a ring of small teeth
round the camera for as long as the access lasts, and sink into the hole when it stops. An access
answered with empty data opens the mark in its own ochre and bites shut into the hole.

### MOT-6 The back gesture draws a line that becomes a dart

- Home: Settings > Accessibility > Color & motion > Lujarch motion
- Switch: Back gesture: on, off
- Default: on
- Verify: vm

Instead of Android's arrow panel, a line of the navigation handle's color is pulled out of the
edge and bows like a thread; when the gesture arms it buckles into an arrowhead aimed at the edge,
disarmed it unfolds back into the edge, and on going back it is cocked into a dart and launched
through the edge. Android's thresholds, springs and states drive it.

### MOT-7 The volume slider holds water and a float

- Home: Settings > Accessibility > Color & motion > Lujarch motion
- Switch: Volume: on, off
- Default: on
- Verify: vm

In the volume dialog the slider's filled part is water that sloshes with each step and with the
dialog's slide, and a shard of the mark floats in it point down, swaying and bobbing with the
level; as the level drains, the shard touches the bottom, stands, and lies down.
