# Status bar

Everything the status bar can show has a switch in one place: every icon, chip, indicator and text
of the status bar, the lock screen's status bar and the shade header, and the lyrics of the song
that plays.

## Items

### SB-1 Every item the status bar can show has a switch

- Home: Settings > System > Status bar > Status bar items
- Switch: each item: on, off
- Default: on each
- Verify: vm

Status bar items lists, in groups (Connectivity, Sound and modes, Status, Activity chips, Privacy
indicators, Clock, battery and notifications), every icon, chip and text the status bar can show on
this phone, each with one switch; an item the phone cannot show is not listed. Off, the item does
not show in the status bar, on the lock screen's status bar or in the shade header, and a chip that
is off does not take its place from another chip. Lujarch's own items (the proxy's icon) are items
of the list like Android's.

### SB-2 Privacy indicators are the owner's choice

- Home: Settings > System > Status bar > Status bar items > Privacy indicators
- Switch: Privacy chip, Privacy dot, Privacy ring, Location: on, off each
- Default: on each
- Verify: vm

The privacy indicators show when an app uses the camera, the microphone or, where the phone shows
it, the location: the chip announces it, the ring around the camera cutout or the dot stays while
it lasts. Each switch says what turning it off lets apps do unseen, and turns off without a
confirmation.

### SB-3 The song's lyrics show in the status bar

- Home: Settings > System > Status bar > Status bar items > Lyrics
- Switch: Lyrics: on, off
- Default: on
- Verify: vm

While a song plays whose app gives its lyrics, the current line scrolls on the left of the status
bar in place of the notification icons, and a tap opens the player.
