# Icons

Every app icon has the shape of all others, and every notification icon is a silhouette, without
the apps doing anything; the owner can keep any app's own icon.

## App icons

### ICON-1 Legacy app icons are fitted to the icon shape

- Home: Settings > Wallpaper & style; Settings > Apps > (app) > Icon
- Switch: Wallpaper & style > Fit app icons: on, off; each app: Icon: Original, Fitted, Redrawn
- Default: Fit app icons: on; each app: Redrawn
- Verify: vm

An app whose icon is a single picture instead of Android's layered icon gets an icon in the shape
of all others, wherever Android shows app icons. Redrawn draws its logo in one color on a plain
ground and gives it a themed version for Themed icons; Fitted places the picture on a ground of its
own colors; a picture that is not a logo is fitted when Redrawn is chosen. Original keeps the app's
own icon. App info > Icon shows the three with previews. Layered icons are never changed; with Fit
app icons off, every app shows its own icon and Icon is not shown.

## Notification icons

### ICON-2 Notification icons are silhouettes

- Home: Settings > Notifications
- Switch: Notifications > Fit notification icons: on, off
- Default: on
- Verify: vm

An app whose notification icon shows as a filled shape, because it is a colored picture where
Android expects a silhouette, shows a silhouette of its logo in the status bar and its
notifications instead, for the apps the system's set of such icons covers. Off, every app shows
its own notification icon.
