# Processor

The Xiaomi 13's processor runs its idle cores at the lowest frequency the chip has.

## Frequency

### CPU-1 Idle cores run at the lowest frequency

- Home: Settings > Battery
- Switch: Settings > Battery > Lowest CPU frequency when idle: on, off
- Default: on
- Verify: phone

On, the floor of each processor cluster is the lowest frequency the chip has, from every start and
at once when the switch changes; off, it is the device's own floor. Touch and app launch boosts are
the same either way.
