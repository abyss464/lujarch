# Launcher

The owner groups the app drawer into sections of their own.

## App drawer

### LCH-1 The owner makes sections in the app drawer

- Home: the app drawer; Home settings > App drawer sections
- Switch: none
- Default: no sections
- Verify: vm

The owner makes a section by dragging an app below the drawer's last row and moves apps into,
out of and between sections by dragging. A section shows under the drawer's apps with a header
that carries the name the owner gives it; a tap on the header folds or opens the section, and a
long press on it renames it, moves it up or down or deletes it, which puts its apps back with the
others. App drawer sections lists them for the same actions. A section exists while it holds apps.
The system makes, names and fills no section by itself, and search finds the apps of folded
sections.
